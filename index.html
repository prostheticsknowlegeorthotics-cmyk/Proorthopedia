📖 Quick Glossary
Residual Limb
The remaining portion of a limb after amputation.
Socket
Custom-fitted interface between the residual limb and prosthesis.
AFO
Ankle-Foot Orthosis — controls ankle and foot motion.
K-Level
Medicare Functional Classification (K0–K4) for ambulatory potential.
Pylon
Structural tube connecting socket to the prosthetic foot.
